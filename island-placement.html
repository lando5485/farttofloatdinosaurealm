This layout is a proposal, not the live game. Dinosaur Realm currently runs 13 slots topping out at Y 19,200. Going to 14 needs an island14 model in Workspace, a 14th entry in SLOT_POS and SLOT_TO_ISLAND, a food for model 14, and the hard-coded 13 updated in Bootstrap, AnchorIslands, IslandTeleport, MonsoonRain, MeteoriteExplosion and WormholeService.

Food power still does not ramp with the climb. The foods table is keyed by island model number while the tower reorders the islands, so along this route power runs 8 → 300 → 25 → 540 → 45 → 640 → 70 → 370 → 100 → 240 → 140 → ? → 185 → 450. Chili Peppers, Grapes and Popcorn each unlock on a slot where your tank is too small to hold one serving, so the purchase is rejected outright until several guts later.
